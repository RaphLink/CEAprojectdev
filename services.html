How My Process Works

  1. Tell Me About Your Projects

    Complete the Get Started form and describe what you're trying to accomplish. You don't need to know exactly what technology you need. Just tell me about your idea or the problem you're trying to solve.

  2. Let's Talk

    I'll review you request and respond within two business days to discuss the next steps. We can talk about your goals, budget, timeline, and possible approaches.

    Consultation are availabe by appointment, primarily during evenings and weekends.

  3. Review Your Project Proposal

    Before work begins, I'll prepare a written Project Proposal explaining

    • The purpose and goals of the project
    • The work to be completed
    • Website pages, features, and design direction, when applicable
    • Estimated project timeline
    • Total project price and payment schedule
    • What's included and excluded

    For websites projects, the proposal may include mockups, design examples, or other visual references so you know what we're planning to build. You can request changes before approving it.

  4. Approve the Agreement

    Once you're satisfied with the proposed plan, we'll review and sign the Project Services Agreement and Project Proposal. These establish what will be built, the agreed price, payment schedule, and how changes or revisions will be handled.

    Development begins after the required initial payment is received.

  5. Build & Review

    I'll develop the project according to the approval proposal and keep you informed as work progresses. You'll have an opportunity to review the work and request corrections if something doesn't meet the agreed requirements.

    Significant changes outside the approved proposal may require an updated quote and written approval.

  6. Complete & Deliver

    Once the agreed requirements have been completed and any verified deficiencies have been addressed, we'll follow the payment and delivery terms in the agreement.

    For websites, this may include final testing, launch, and transfer of the agreed deliverables.

Clear Expectations. No Surprises.

Before any work begins, you'll receive a written agreement. The approved Project Proposal becomes part of that agreement.

This helps ensure we're working toward the same goals and that the completed project can be reviewed against the requirements we agreed upon.

If you request changes after approving the proposal, I'll discuss any additional costs or timeline adjustments with you before proceeding.

Your written agreement outlines:

  • The work to be completed
  • The total project price
  • The payment schedule
  • The estimated timeline
  • What's included and excluded
  • Revision allowances
  • Project ownership and delivery terms
  • How changes and payment issues are handled
